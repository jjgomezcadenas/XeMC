\subsection*{Group ranking}

By total annual rate in the ROI:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Others}: $6.42$~cts/yr --- dominated by cavern walls
      ($4.24$~cts/yr) and the LZ outer-detector liquid scintillator
      ($1.02$~cts/yr).
\item \textbf{PMTs}: $3.57$~cts/yr --- spread roughly evenly across
      TPC PMTs, structures, and cables.
\item \textbf{Field Cage}: $2.11$~cts/yr --- dominated by FC resistors
      ($0.97$~cts/yr) despite their tiny mass (60~g), reflecting the
      very high specific activity ($1350$~mBq/kg $^{238}$U-late,
      $2010$~mBq/kg $^{232}$Th-late).
\item \textbf{Cryostat}: $0.89$~cts/yr --- the cleanest detector
      subsystem by total contribution.
\end{enumerate}

The grand total $12.99$~cts/yr matches the LZ projection of
$\sim 13$ background events per year in the $\pm 1\sigma$ ROI.
